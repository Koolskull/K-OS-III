# Aquino, MindWar, Temple of Set, and the Presidio investigation

## Short version

**Michael A. Aquino** (1946–2019) was a U.S. Army officer (retired Lt. Col.), psychological operations / military intelligence specialist, and founder of the **Temple of Set** (1975 split from the Church of Satan). He co-authored the open 1980 staff study *From PSYOP to MindWar: The Psychology of Victory* with Col. Paul Vallely. That paper is **not** a documented MKUltra sub-project named "Operation Mind War."

In 1986–87 the **Presidio of San Francisco** daycare abuse scandal centered on daycare worker **Gary Hambright**. Aquino and his wife Lilith were later named after a child's identification of "Mikey" and "Shamby." **San Francisco Police closed their investigation of the Aquinos for insufficient evidence (Sept 1988); no criminal charges were filed against Aquino.** Army CID continued and "titled" him in a report he litigated (*Aquino v. Stone*, 4th Cir. 1992). The FBI participated in the joint investigation — that is **investigative involvement**, not evidence that the FBI paid him to torture, rape, or blackmail people as a standing program.

**Unsupported as established fact:** that Aquino was paid by the FBI (or any agency) to torture, rape, and blackmail people for data that "never proved helpful." That claim exceeds the public documentary record and must be labeled as such under this project's epistemic stance.

## Expanded notes

### Documented career and Temple of Set

- Army PSYOP/Special Forces training pipeline; Vietnam-era service; later Active Guard Reserve work connected to the Presidio; teaching; reserve/intelligence-related schooling in secondary bios.
- Open about Setianism while holding a clearance — the reason media and conspiracy circuits fixed on him.
- Temple of Set: Left-Hand Path initiatory organization; primary texts include materials associated with *The Book of Coming Forth by Night* and the Crystal Tablet tradition as presented by the Temple.

### MindWar paper

- Title: *From PSYOP to MindWar: The Psychology of Victory* (1980), Vallely & Aquino, 7th PSYOP Group, Presidio of San Francisco.
- Content (in Aquino's later framing): unclassified staff study urging more sophisticated PSYOP doctrine after Vietnam frustration — not a classified torture manual.
- Internet lore renames this into "Operation Mind War" under MKUltra/Paperclip. **No verified government program under that operational name matches the legend.** Read the paper; do not substitute the title alone for a program history.

### Presidio investigation — timeline that matters

1. **Late 1986:** Allegations of sexual abuse at the Army Child Development Center; Hambright becomes central suspect; medical and parental reports of harm to multiple children appear in contemporary coverage.
2. **Aug 1987:** Chaplain's young daughter reportedly identifies Aquino and Lilith after a PX encounter; FBI agent interview expands investigation to include them.
3. **SFPD:** Closes Aquino investigation **Sept 1988**, insufficient evidence; no charges.
4. **Army CID:** Continues; issues report placing Aquino in the title block; statute of limitations / uncooperative leads noted in court summary; Aquino challenges the report.
5. **Criminal charges against Aquino:** **Never filed.**

Aquino's defense (and some later secondary summaries, e.g. Mitch Horowitz): for key windows of the original allegation timeline he and Lilith were in **Washington, D.C.** on duty/study. CID materials and accusatory books dispute methods and weight of child statements. **No criminal conviction.**

The daycare case itself involved real reported harm and parental agony. That is separate from proving Aquino's guilt in a criminal court.

### What the FBI record supports vs. what it does not

| Supported | Not supported by public record |
| --- | --- |
| Joint SFPD / FBI / Army CID investigation of Presidio daycare allegations | FBI payroll for Aquino to commit rape/torture/blackmail |
| FBI Vault FOIA material under Aquino's name (investigative records are normal for high-profile subjects) | "Data harvest" program proven from such crimes |
| Child identification and CID titling without criminal charge | Johnny Gosch purchase / trafficking command narratives as adjudicated fact |
| Open MindWar PSYOP doctrine paper | MindWar = MKUltra operational torture program |

### How stronger internet narratives form

1. Real: Army PSYOP officer + open occult leadership + flashy *MindWar* title.
2. Real: Presidio daycare scandal + child ID + CID titling without charge.
3. Real: 1980s Satanic Panic context (recovered-memory / ritual-abuse claims that later failed in many jurisdictions).
4. **Leap:** therefore FBI-paid rapist/torturer for worthless data.

Steps 1–3 are discussable with documents. Step 4 is not established by those documents.

**Secondary / accusatory (advocacy, not verdicts)**

- Linda Blood, *The New Satanists*; Carl Raschke, *Painted Black* — Aquino reported out-of-court settlements; treat as named secondary accusations.
- Conspiracy literature linking Aquino to Johnny Gosch and similar cases — allegations; no matching criminal conviction of Aquino on those narratives in the public record used here.

**How to use**

Read the Fourth Circuit opinion and the MindWar paper first. Then read one accusatory book and Aquino's rebuttal. Note where they agree on the existence of an investigation and where they diverge on guilt. Do not upgrade uncharged internet claims into "paid by the FBI to rape."
