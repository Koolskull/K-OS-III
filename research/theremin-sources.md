# THEREMIN_SOURCES.md

Sources for `THEREMIN_REPORT.md`, compiled 6 October 2026 (PT).

- This list includes only URLs I actually opened or downloaded, unless a source is marked **snippet only** or is listed under "Searched for and not found / not accessible."
- I cite no YouTube videos and no pundits or commentators.
- Local copies of the PDFs and their text extractions are in `src/theremin/` on the box.

**Status labels for sources.**

- **PRIMARY**: the inventor's own writing, a patent, a contemporary press record, an official agency or museum record, or a designer's own technical document.
- **FIRST-PERSON**: a direct account by a participant (an interview or memoir).
- **SECONDARY-STRONG**: a scholarly or well-sourced secondary account.
- **SECONDARY**: a reputable journalistic or reference account.
- **SNIPPET ONLY**: seen only as a search-result excerpt. It is not relied on for any VERIFIED claim.
