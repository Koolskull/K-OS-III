# Claim sorter

> Ships with Lesson 01 (use throughout the track). Not quiz‑gated — open anytime.

Use this worksheet to keep claims, sources, and verification status straight.

| Claim | Who says it (named position) | Source + medium | Status | What would change the status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Example: “1 Cor 1:18 calls the cross ‘foolishness’” | P6 Pauline‑exegetical | Bible Gateway (KJV/ESV/NIV) — primary | VERIFIED | — |
| Example: “Symeon dragged a dead dog” | P1 Orthodox hagiographic | Leontius, Life (Krueger trans.) — hagiography | HAGIOGRAPHY | Independent contemporaneous attestation |
| Example: “Andrew existed as described” | P1 vs P3 | Rydén (text), scholars (dating) | CONTESTED | New philological evidence on authorship/date |
| Example: “Matrona of Moscow was a fool‑for‑Christ” | Framing by later writers | Pravoslavie.ru + canonization acts | CLAIMED (framing); DOCUMENTED (status ≠ “fool”) | Formal title change (unlikely) |
| Example: “The 1963 burning monk was pure politics” | Mainstream press vs Hoang Ngo 2023 | Kyoto Review 2023; press timelines | CONTESTED | Authoritative Buddhist sources ruling out the Lotus Sutra frame |

Status tags:
- VERIFIED — primary source or two independent reliable sources.
- SUPPORTED — one reliable source.
- HAGIOGRAPHY — a Life/tradition reports it; no independent corroboration.
- CONTESTED — named scholars disagree; present both sides.
- CLAIMED — unattributed anecdote or retelling.
- UNVERIFIED — searched and not confirmed.
- CONTRADICTED — reliable sources say otherwise.
- NOT ADJUDICATED — interpretive question the track declines to settle.

Medium tags:
- primary document · critical edition · translation (translator named) · liturgical text · secondary scholarship · official · press · fan · machine transcript · 🗣️ owner dictation.

Keep the line “don’t run off cliffs or light yourself on fire” labeled as OWNER’S FRAME wherever you log it.
