# 04 — Value that compounds

> **Track:** Creativity as the Way Out · **Lesson 4 of 6**
> **Tools shipped with this lesson:** `tools/compounding-sketch.md`

---

## What this lesson is for

Real growth is exponential. Exponential growth is the signature of something that produces. Compounding is not a finance trick — it is what creation looks like when it persists.

This lesson translates that into something you can count without finance jargon. The point is **reuse**, not interest rates. A thing that can be reused or shared multiplies. A thing that cannot is consumed once and gone.

---

## The compounding sketch

Open `tools/compounding-sketch.md` and fill in the small table:

| What I made | How many times it can be reused or shared | What that multiplies |
|---|---|---|
| (one row per build) | (a sample in another track, a sprite in another animation, a paragraph in another document) | (the rough multiple) |

One row is enough. The sketch is not an accounting exercise. It is a way of seeing that the value of a build is not only in the making — it is in everything the making can become again.

---

## Why this matters for the trauma-shaped reader

A nervous system shaped by permanent negative experience tends to treat every output as final and every failure as total. Compounding is the counter-pattern: nothing you make is only what it is today. It is also every future thing it can feed. That is not optimism. It is arithmetic.
