> Ships with Lesson 05 (use with Lesson 04). Not quiz‑gated — open anytime.

Pick one dossier (e.g., St Gabriel 1965). Read it five ways. Note the evidence each reading relies on and what it leaves out.

Guides:
- Chosen vs imputed: who ascribes the “madness,” the ascetic or the state?
- Illness: speculative without specific evidence; handle with humility.
- Performance: Ivanov’s audience‑construction model.
- Legend: late Lives; edifying edits and uncheckable episodes.
