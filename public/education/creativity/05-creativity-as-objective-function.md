# 05 — Creativity as the objective function

> **Track:** Creativity as the Way Out · **Lesson 5 of 6**
> **Tools shipped with this lesson:** `tools/weekly-build-log.md`

---

## What this lesson is for

This is the tract's strongest claim, taken from the source material: for long-term investing and exponential growth, creativity is the primary objective function, and this holds under any mathematical observation from any worldview.

The reasoning: every durable system of value — biological, economic, cultural, spiritual — is a system that produces more than it consumes. Production at that scale is indistinguishable from creation. Whether one calls the source God or calls it emergence, the mathematics of compounding reward the builder.

---

## The weekly build log

Open `tools/weekly-build-log.md`. One row per week, three fields:

1. **Built** — what was made.
2. **Reused** — where it showed up again.
3. **Compounded** — what that reuse multiplied.

This is the tract's long-term instrument. It makes the objective-function claim visible over time instead of leaving it as an assertion. Fill it whether or not you feel like it. The log does not measure your worth. It measures your output — and output is the only variable the tract claims you can control.

---

## Why "any worldview"

The claim does not require you to accept a particular metaphysics. A Christian reader sees creation as participation in God's ongoing work. A secular reader sees compounding as the mathematics of anything that produces. Both are looking at the same pattern: systems that produce more than they consume persist and grow; systems that consume more than they produce collapse. The weekly log is where you watch that pattern in your own work.
