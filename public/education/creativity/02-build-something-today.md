# 02 — Build something today

> **Track:** Creativity as the Way Out · **Lesson 2 of 6**
> **Tools shipped with this lesson:** `tools/first-build-template.md`

---

## What this lesson is for

Not motivation. Not inspiration. **Production.** The smallest real build: one bar of music, one sprite, one paragraph, one line of code. The point is that it is external and verifiable.

This lesson exists because lesson 01 named the opening, and an opening that is never walked through closes again. The practice is deliberately small. A first build is allowed to be small. The only requirement is that "done" means someone else could see it — not that it is good.

---

## The first-build template

Open `tools/first-build-template.md` and fill in the three fields:

1. **What I will build** — one sentence, specific. "A four-bar loop." "A 16x16 sprite of a skull." "One paragraph about why I started this."
2. **What I built** — one sentence, honest. Not what you intended. What actually exists now.
3. **What it proves** — one sentence about *agency*, not about quality. "I can start something and finish it." "I made a decision and followed it."

If you are inside K-OS, the build can live in any tool you already have: Datamoshpit for a bar of music, KoolDraw for a sprite, any text surface for a paragraph. The template does not care which surface. It cares that the three fields get filled.

---

## The minimum viable build

**Done** means: a person other than you could open it, see it, and know it was made by a human who chose to make it. That is the entire bar. Quality is a later conversation. Presence is today's.

If the state named in lesson 01 is loud, lower the bar further. One note. One pixel. One word. The template still works at that scale — the "what it proves" field is where the value lives, and it is available at any size.
