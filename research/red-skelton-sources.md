# SOURCES: Red Skelton research report (2026-10-06)

Each entry gives the key used in `REPORT.md`, the URL, its source type and what it supports. Every page listed was fetched on 2026-10-06, live or as an archived copy, unless the entry says otherwise. Local text extracts are in `raw/`.

## Encyclopedias and reference works
- **[Britannica]**: Britannica Editors, "Red Skelton". https://www.britannica.com/biography/Red-Skelton. Reference.
  - Supports: birth on 7/18/1913 in Vincennes; death on 9/17/1997 in Rancho Mirage; his father's death; newsboy at 7; medicine show at 10; minstrel, burlesque and circus work; the 1937 Vallee show; "outstanding new radio star of 1941"; "some 30 movies"; the TV run 1951–71; the TV Academy Hall of Fame in 1988.
  - Its exact phrasing, "broad humour with emotional complexity", contains no Chaplin claim.
  - It carries the title error "Having a Wonderful Time".
- **[MBC]**: Museum of Broadcast Communications, *Encyclopedia of Television*, "Skelton, Red". Archived at https://web.archive.org/web/20140407060639/http://www.museum.tv/eotv/skeltonred.htm. Reference.
  - Supports: the filmography; "corny gags and slapstick"; the 1970 cancellation for "rising production costs" and "upscale advertisers", with the show seventh in its final season.
- **[StJames]**: *St. James Encyclopedia of Popular Culture*, "Skelton, Red (1913–1997)", on Encyclopedia.com. https://www.encyclopedia.com/media/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/skelton-red-1913-1997. Reference.
  - Supports: the Ross Wetzsteon quote "miss every trend…", as quoted via Maltin, *The Great Movie Comedians*; "more maudlin… losing his audience"; the post-war film flops.
  - Wetzsteon's original: Village Voice, March 14, 1977, per Wikipedia. That scan was not checked.
- **[Gehring-IMH]**: Wes D. Gehring, "Red Skelton and Clem Kadiddlehopper", *Indiana Magazine of History* (March 1996). https://scholarworks.iu.edu/journals/index.php/imh/article/view/11504 (PDF: …/11504/16718). Scholarly.
  - Supports: his mother as a cleaning lady; Clem drawn from Carl Hopper, a hearing-impaired friend; the Leo Rosten quote (n. 18, quoting Arthur Marx, *Red Skelton*, p. 170; the OCR reads "Roster"); the Freddie routine "borrowed directly from Chaplin's *Modern Times*" (n. 21).
- **Wikipedia, "Red Skelton"**: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Skelton (raw wikitext was read). Used only as a pointer.
  - Points to: Hyatt and Gehring for the Groucho quote, burial, Pledge requests, Nixon, Marceau and the Eheart surname; the Lawrence Journal-World (1966) for the circus; the Pharos-Tribune (1997) on "may God bless"; the StarNews (1982) on the sign-off; the 1947 censorship; the 1980 writers' suit; the cancellation complaints; pneumonia; AFI for *Ocean's 11*.
  - Related pages: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Red_Skelton_Show and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rural_purge (the "rural purge" frame, citing classictvguide).

## Obituaries and press
- **[NYT-obit]**: "Red Skelton, Knockabout Comic and Clown Prince of the Airwaves, Is Dead at 84", *New York Times*, 9/18/1997. Archived at https://web.archive.org/web/20140407141458/http://www.nytimes.com/1997/09/18/arts/red-skelton-knockabout-comic-and-clown-prince-of-the-airwaves-is-dead-at-84.html (page 2 from the 20150527055152 snapshot). Press.
  - Supports: his father, a Hagenbeck & Wallace clown; newsboy; seventh grade; *Cotton Blossom*; the Loew's Montreal doughnut act; "Good night, and God bless"; "too sentimental and not sophisticated enough"; "hokey" vs "dirty jokes"; the Oral Roberts joke; "known as a clown"; the paintings ($80,000+; $2.5M a year by his own estimate); "never ranked below the Top 20"; "younger viewers".
  - It names no cause of death.

## Official, institutional and archival
- **[TVAcad]**: Television Academy, "Red Skelton: Hall of Fame Tribute", from the 1988 induction program. https://www.televisionacademy.com/features/news/hall-fame/red-skelton-hall-fame-tribute. Official.
  - Supports: the 1988 induction; his 1957 TV Guide "religious books… make people laugh" remark; quotes from Carson (writer 1953–54), Rose and Peggy Gordon; Freddie "always done in mime"; cutting the biggest laughs; testing material in living rooms; seventh in the Nielsens at cancellation; four hours' sleep; Marceau.
- **SAG Life Achievement Award list (archived)**: https://www.actorawards.org/nominees/life-achievement-award-recipient/24th and the …/25th page. Official (the awarding body). Supports: the award order of Fabray 1986, Skelton, then Gene Kelly 1988, so **1987**.
- **[RS-official]**: Red Skelton estate site, biography. https://www.redskelton.com/biography (also https://www.redskelton.com/museum). Official.
  - Supports: "a man of deep faith"; "58 year membership in the Masons and the Shriners" and the 33rd degree; "Good night and may God bless"; the Clown Hall of Fame; pneumonia; Forest Lawn Glendale; Ed Wynn at age 10.
  - Errors: it puts the Pledge on a "radio show" and says "CBS beckoned" in 1951.
- **[Museum-guide]**: Red Skelton Museum of American Comedy episode guide. https://www.redskeltonmuseum.org/episode-guide/season-2, …/season-5, …/season-11, …/season-18. Official.

- **[Museum-timeline]**: https://www.redskeltonmuseum.org/timelines/1913-1922, …/1923-1929, …/1960-1969, …/1980-1989. Official.
