This page is the inventory and the reference manual. The spreadsheet is catalog.xml. Drawings sit in art/. stardrain.app opens on the hotel at night. Rooms, levels, menus, games, playlists, and the scene are the engine. /mialien/ stays the metadata tree.

cosmology

Toroidal Patterns

From Walter Russell's wave cosmology in The Secret of Light—twin spiral vortices forming figure-eight or toroidal shapes at every scale. These patterns define the structure of the firmament orbs that float in the infinite ocean.

Core Premise: Reality Hierarchy

Round Earth is a programmed projection that beings perceive. The astral plane is an even more illusory layer of thought-forms and emotion-made-tangible. Outside both sits base reality—the actual planes beyond the simulation. Hierarchy: 3D physical > astral illusion > base reality outside both.

Outer Waters Between Orbs

The infinite ocean between firmament orbs is populated by extremely abstract creatures akin to deep-sea anglerfish and other strange liquid-dwellers. These beings float in the ether where creative dynamic possibility exists as pure energy flow. This dimension is the visual source of the paintings.

Each place sits in its own orb. The ocean is the space between them.

This is the Thrash Dimension.

Thrash Dimension

A surrealist realm of floating fish, mutants, and never-ending loops of monsters emerging from the mouths of other monsters. It is not evil or darkness—it symbolizes the infinite possibility of God. The Thrash Dimension sits closer to base reality than the lower hologram layers.

locations

Mediocricity

The city of Mediocricity sits in its own glass-and-steel orb, off the coast of Stardrain. It is far enough that the people in the city do not think of the island. It is run by the record label Smiley Teeth Records, which holds extreme power across many different planets in the lower hologram dimensions.

The orb is a bubble. People do not leave. Underworld plumbing passages are the connection to Stardrain. The public is depressed and lethargic: union workers and pensioners on a weekly SMILE wage, many of them attached to menial bureaucratic jobs they do not understand. The easiest wage is the processing floor. Counters inside the bubble take SMILE and refuse TEEFZ.

Stardrain Island

A cracked, broken fragment of land that split from a larger body thousands of years ago. Stardrain is its own orb. Mediocricity is not on it. North side: mountains, beach, garden, temple to the green slime. South side: abandoned buildings and the Stardrain Hotel.

South Side of Stardrain Island

Filled with abandoned and broken buildings that Hollywood Cowboy has not yet refurbished because he prioritized building the Stardrain Hotel. He wants hotel residents to start their own businesses so the south side can be restored and developed.

North Side of Stardrain Island

Mountains, beach, and a large garden containing Hollywood Cowboy's temple to the green slime (the power source for his plants). The Stardrain Hotel sits in the lower middle-right center of the island, facing south. First floor: large market mall + two music venues. Upper floors: apartments. Hollywood and family live at the top.

Above the hotel rises a mountain that symbolizes the Hollywood Hills, complete with a “Star Drain” sign styled after the Hollywood sign, a radio station, unique caves, and beaches on the northernmost edge.

characters

Hollywood Cowboy

Protagonist who runs the Stardrain Hotel. He is the island's landlord, mayor, and lead botanist. An ex-rock star, he put his career winnings into private research and grew plants that store decentralized cloud data in their genetic structure. That is why storage on the island is treated as infinite. He draws infinite energy directly from the heavenly realms and is therefore independent of the Mediocricity power grid. He lives with his family at the very top of the hotel.

systems

Smiley Teeth Records

An extremely powerful record label operating in the dimension of darkness. It owns large portions of colonized planets' governments and corporate structures in the lower hologram reality (Star Trek-like black space with planets and stars). Because it exists in darkness, it constantly requires sacrifices to operate and maintain power.

The label issues SMILE and the Smiley Tooth card used inside Mediocricity. The card balance makes change. Paper notes, such as NOTE-777, are exact inventory. Some counters take the notes and refuse the card. The clone economy is unchanged: see Sacrifice Mechanism.

Sacrifice Mechanism

Smiley Teeth Records kills its pop stars and replaces them with clones—not merely for efficiency and profit maximization, but to fuel the black magic that sustains the label. This truth is only suggested early in the story and is discovered late.

Multi-Dimensional Internet

The island's internet is multi-dimensional. Artists push their music and art not only across different planets but also across different timelines and dimensions by tapping into the weird layers of reality available from the Thrash Dimension.

Some of those sites ask for a currency the visitor does not hold. TEEFZ spends on the island. SMILE spends in the bubble. A site from another dimension may want neither. The rate is not posted. See Exchanges.

culture

Modular Plants

Genetically mutated botany created by Hollywood Cowboy. The fruit and vegetables function as modular audio and video units that can be arranged exactly like Eurorack modules and modular synthesizers. Residents of the island build music, video, and art from these plants and sell the work commercially across planets.

PADPEAR grows on trees. Oscillators fruit as berries: BERRY-SINE, BERRY-SQUARE, BERRY-SAW, BERRY-PULSE, and BERRY-NOISE. VINE-CABLE grows as a vine. The rack continues in DRUM-MODULE, SAMPLER-POD, SEQUENCER-STALK, MIXER-BLOSSOM, FILTER-FROND, TAPE-REEL, CD-FRUIT, and SYNTH-VOICE. Each of those is its own exact good. A saw berry is not change for a sine berry.

The plants store data in their genes. A rig on the island can keep growing storage without a stated cap. The field manual lists every cultivar.

economy

TEEFZ

TEEFZ is the currency of Stardrain Island and the Stardrain Hotel. It is worthless at Mediocricity counters. Change mode is on for the whole group, so the hotel will break a note into smaller faces that sum to the same number of teef.

Faces, in teef: TEEF is 1, JAW is 16, MOUF is 32, SKULL is 128, and SKELETONNE is 1024. Two jaws make a mouf. Four moufs make a skull. Eight skulls make a SkeleTonne. Pocket coins and the digitized balance use this same table. Faces above SkeleTonne are reserved and unused.

The TEEFZ logo is a ghost. That ghost is the default picture on TEEF. A separate sheet of 64 drawings, eight across and eight down, is more pictures of that same coin. Those drawings are not extra denominations, and the first cell is not the logo. Jaw, mouf, skull, and SkeleTonne do not have their own drawing yet. Slime credits can be exchanged for these coins. The rate is unset.

This is the Stardrain reading of a multidenominational token. The contract is not published yet. The catalog records the faces so a later deployment can follow them.

SMILE

SMILE is the Smiley Tooth token. Smiley Teeth Records issues it to Mediocricity. The chain stores integer cents. One cent is shown as 0.01 SMILE. The card faces are SMILE-001, SMILE-005, SMILE-025, SMILE-050, SMILE-1, SMILE-4, SMILE-11, SMILE-44, SMILE-88, SMILE-222, and SMILE-777.

The Smiley Tooth card is change mode. A wage from the processing floor posts to the card, which sits in the character's wallet. The same eleven amounts exist as paper notes, from NOTE-001 through NOTE-777. A note is exact inventory. A shop that takes paper will not shatter a 777 note into cents, and a shop that takes the card may refuse the note.

The label's bank is where a card balance becomes notes and notes become a card balance. TEEFZ does not buy SMILE inside either token. That trade is an exchange, when someone will make it. Slime credits can be exchanged for SMILE the same way. The mouth is the SMILE logo, the mark on every face. Larger production art is still empty.

Character Wallet

A new player makes a character. That character is the save. It is an ERC-721, and ERC-6551 gives the token its own wallet, the same pattern as the Sanko buns. The wallet starts empty. Clothes, the top-view body, and any later model are metadata on the character, with the same picture sizes and model slots as a tool.

The player moves TEEFZ, SMILE, slime credits, gear, MiyaMaker pieces, and finished works between their own wallet and any character wallet they own. The same holdings are what the account shows on 2kool.me and miyamaker.com. See Shared Account. Selling the character sells the wallet with it. Whatever is still inside — coins, notes, tools, works — goes to the buyer, and the listing has to show those holdings first. Holdings are why a seller can ask more. The seller sets the price. The market does not price a character from the contents, because a drum module is not cash.

A listing does not take custody of the character. A vault that held the NFT would hold the wallet too. Nothing here deploys the account. This page is the rule the contract will have to meet.

Exchanges

TEEFZ and SMILE do not convert inside either token. On the island, the hotel makes change in TEEFZ. In the bubble, the Smiley Tooth bank makes change on the card and will swap the card for notes.

Between the island and the bubble, and onward into other dimensions, the trade is a person, a desk, or a site. Some of those desks are outside Mediocricity law. The city does not post their rates. The player finds them by playing. A site on the multi-dimensional internet may ask for a coin the visitor does not hold. The catalog does not contain a rate.

Slime credits trade for TEEF and for SMILE-1 at those same desks. The three groups stay separate contracts unless Zodomo's flag can host more than one group. A slime coin is not a teef, and a teef is not a SMILE cent.

Exact Goods

Tools are the same multidenominational standard as the coins, with exact inventory turned on. Copies of one tool stack. They do not break into other tools. You can buy a drum machine. You cannot take it back and receive three cheaper drum machines, unless that counter's own policy says so. Most counters are not that gracious, and a tool is not as liquid as cash.

DRUM-MODULE stays a drum module. BERRY-SAW is not three BERRY-SINE. ISLAND-DRUM-PACK is bought with TEEFZ on the island. LABEL-DRUM-PACK is bought with SMILE in Mediocricity. Same function, different city, different token, not interchangeable. YM2612-CHIP and BROADCAST-LIMITER are city goods. The limiter is what the bubble requires before it will air a piece.

Prices are unset. The field manual holds the function, the zone, and the currency for each row. Art fills the picture slots later. The function stays put when a new engine ships new art.

Craft

These burn rules are not final. No recipe amounts are set, and no contract is written.

The working rule is a one-way craft. The player, through the character wallet, burns exact-inventory tools and receives one ERC-721 work: a customized instrument, a track, or a picture. The work's metadata records the tools that were burned and a hash of the player's settings. The audio itself stays off the chain.

Ingredients in the current sheet include PADPEAR, VINE-CABLE, MIXER-BLOSSOM, FILTER-FROND, the oscillator berries, SAMPLER-POD, SEQUENCER-STALK, DRUM-MODULE, SYNTH-VOICE, TAPE-REEL, and CD-FRUIT. Other gear is marked as a fixture until the review says it can be burned. TEEFZ and SMILE can be a fee. They are not ingredients, and a jaw is not a substitute for a berry.

A work is not a character and does not get its own wallet. It can sit in the character's wallet, and it stays out of a sale if the player moves it first. Turning the work back into the original tools would be a different product. It stays off until that review says otherwise.

The resemblance to a dual token is only the result the player sees: several ERC-1155s become one unique token. The standard for the coins does not have to grow a burn hook for this. That question is open.

Slime Credits

Slime credits are the token of 2kool.me. They use the circle family of Slimentologika, the eight glyphs S0 through S7 defined in the K-OS guide. In the tracker those glyphs are the digits 0 through 7. On the token, S0 stays zero and is not a coin. The eight coins are SLIME-S1 through SLIME-S7, worth 1 through 7, and SLIME-S10, worth 8, written S1 S0. Change mode is on, so seven S1 and one more S1 are one S10.

The square family, S8 through SF, stays the tracker's upper digits. It is not a second set of coins.

You earn slime by working in the tools and by releasing a project in public. Finishing one piece credits enough slime to cover the gas of that account's first public mint on 2kool.me, whether the piece is a song, an image, a drumkit, or a video. The credit is sized to that mint. It is not a quoted gas price, and it is not ETH sitting in a K-OS account. Later mints are paid by the person minting. How the first transaction is broadcast is still open.

Slime spends as an exchange into TEEF or SMILE-1. It is not accepted as itself at the hotel or inside the bubble. Tool use still does not require an account. The credit attaches when the work is tied to the shared account.

Shared Account

One Koolskull account is the person on 2kool.me, on miyamaker.com, and in Stardrain. The account that already exists is the one: Sign-In With Ethereum, or Matrix, with extra methods linked only if the person wants them. The tools keep working with no account at all.

When the account is there, the same page can show slime credits, TEEFZ, SMILE, the character wallet, Miyamaker Mini, MiyaMaker songs and kits, and a public mint of a song, an image, a drumkit, or a video. Miyamaker Mini is the existing ERC-721 collection. A new public mint is a separate work. Holding one does not become the other.

A Stardrain character can hold those same tokens in its own wallet, and the account can show what the character holds. Selling the character still sells only what is inside the character wallet. Moving a song out to the personal wallet before the sale keeps it. None of this merges the three monies into one contract, and none of it puts K-OS in custody of the coins.

Processing Floor

The processing floor is the menial wage of Mediocricity. It is a job, not a catalog row, and it is not built yet. This is the rule sheet.

The room is top-down. A conveyor runs on the left. A truck bay sits on the right. One worker moves on a tile grid. The belt feeds orthogonal rectangles. The worker carries one piece, rotates it in quarter turns, and sets it in the truck's grid before the next piece is due. Miss the window, or place a piece that does not fit, and that piece drops. The shift still pays a base pension. A clean pack pays more.

Pay posts to the Smiley Tooth card as SMILE, in the character's wallet, not as paper notes. The drawings for the worker, the belt, and the boxes come later. They are top-view art on top of this grid.
