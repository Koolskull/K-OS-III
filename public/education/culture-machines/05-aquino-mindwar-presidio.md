# Aquino, MindWar, Temple of Set, and the Presidio investigation

Some men collect medals. Some collect initiations. Michael A. Aquino collected both—and then collected the kind of internet legend that outruns court dockets. This lesson keeps the camera on **paper**: the PSYOP study, the Temple, the Presidio investigation, and the claims that still fail to become convictions.

{{illust:mindwar-field|Broadcast fields rippling — MindWar as doctrine imagery, not a torture cam.}}

## Short version

- Aquino (1946–2019): U.S. Army officer (Lt. Col., ret.), PSYOP / intelligence specialist, founder of the **Temple of Set** (1975).
- Co-authored *From PSYOP to MindWar: The Psychology of Victory* (1980) with Col. Paul Vallely—an open staff study, **not** a verified MKUltra sub-project named "Operation Mind War."
- Presidio daycare scandal (1986–87) centered on Gary Hambright; Aquino later named after a child’s identification. **SFPD closed the Aquino investigation Sept 1988 for insufficient evidence; no criminal charges against Aquino.** Army CID titled him; he litigated (*Aquino v. Stone*, 4th Cir. 1992).
- FBI joint investigation ≠ FBI payroll to torture/rape/blackmail. That stronger claim is **unsupported** as established fact under this project’s stance.

## Two dossiers that must not be glued

**Dossier A — MindWar.** Read the paper. It argues for sophisticated psychological operations after Vietnam frustration. Internet lore renames it into a secret torture brand. Title ≠ operational cryptonym history.

{{illust:dossier-stamp|A page under a restless stamp — investigation as paperwork weather.}}

**Dossier B — Presidio.** Real reported harm to children. Real investigation. Real panic-era context (1980s ritual-abuse prosecutions that later failed in many places). Real absence of criminal conviction for Aquino. CID titling is not a jury verdict.
