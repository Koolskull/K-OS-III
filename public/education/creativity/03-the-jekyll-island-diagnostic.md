## What this lesson is for

The 1910 Jekyll Island meeting produced the Aldrich Plan and, ultimately, the Federal Reserve. Sovereignty lesson 06 tells that story with primary sources and competing accounts. This lesson takes the same thread one level deeper: from *what happened on the island* to *what the island reveals about value itself*.

The tract's test, stated in the source material: **if it is real, it has value; if it is a facade, it will not maintain long-term value, because there is nothing inside it.**

Finance, on this reading, is not exclusively the product of hidden bloodlines or occult machinations. It is a mirror of a deeper pattern — continuous creation. On the Christian reading, God is constantly creating, building newer, bigger, better things at an exponential rate. On the secular reading, the same pattern appears as compounding. The men on Jekyll Island were not inventing value; they were institutionalizing a mirror of it.

## The value diagnostic

Open `tools/value-diagnostic.md`. It is a three-column worksheet. Fill one row per subject — a project, a relationship, a self-image belief:

| Column 1: What is inside it | Column 2: What is claimed about it | Column 3: What time has already shown |
|---|---|---|
| The real content — the code, the song, the relationship, the belief | What others say, what you say, what the market says | Does it still stand? Does it still get used? Does it still matter? |

The instruction is not to judge. Only to observe the gap between column one and column two. A facade collapses under time. A real thing compounds. Your job in this lesson is only to see which one you are looking at.
