# Vita check

> Ships with Lessons 02–03 (and 05). Not quiz‑gated — open anytime.

Source‑distance and status worksheet for any saint’s Life (`vita`) or dossier.

1) Source distance
- Author named? Edition and translator named?
- When was the text composed relative to the figure’s life?
- What is the stated purpose (liturgical, edifying, political)?
- Is there independent attestation (chronicles, inscriptions, archives)?
- Earliest manuscript or critical edition?

2) Status table

3) Miracle claims
- Record separately. Tag as HAGIOGRAPHY unless independently attested.

4) Guardrails
- Do not diagnose in the track’s own voice.
- Name who says what, with sources, and label the status.
