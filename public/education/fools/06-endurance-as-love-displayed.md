## What this lesson is for

Synthesis. Name the owner’s frame plainly and test it against lessons 01–05. Close with the two doors and the call to build.

🗣️ OWNER’S FRAME (interpretation; labeled, not doctrine):

- “Saints who were unusually enduring of bizarre experiences in order to display their love for Christ.” Endurance is love made visible. Most readers are not called to humiliation or death; most are called to build — creation before feeling.

Checkpoints against the record:

- Lesson 01: the “foolishness” is the world’s verdict; discernment separates obedience from recklessness (Polycarp 4; Clement; Elvira 60; Augustine — VERIFIED).
- Lesson 02–03: many Eastern cases are hagiographic literature; some Russian cases stand on stronger chronicles; formal statuses vary (DOCUMENTED vs HAGIOGRAPHY).
- Lesson 04–05: the Soviet century shows “imputed madness” (punitive psychiatry) beside chosen folly; “foolishness unto death” among missionaries belongs to a different category and returns us to the discernment line.

Where the frame fits well (SUPPORTED):

- Gabriel’s parrhesia and confinement; Nicholas’s Pskov story (with weaker sourcing); Xenia’s long veneration. These read as endurance.

Where it must not be over‑read (NOT ADJUDICATED):

- Inner states and diagnoses. The track does not adjudicate sanctity or illness.
