# Grateful Dead, Acid Tests, and academic LSD

The lights are wrong on purpose. The juice may or may not be. A band that is still learning its own name is asked to become weather. Somewhere upstream of the party, a research ward had already opened a door—and a generation walked through it carrying guitars.

{{illust:acid-kaleido|Kaleidoscope test pattern — Acid Test as moving geometry, not a travel poster.}}

## Short version

- LSD’s modern story begins with Hofmann / Sandoz; early U.S. use included **academic and government-linked research**.
- Ken Kesey encountered LSD through federally connected research settings before the Merry Pranksters’ public **Acid Tests** (1965–66).
- The **Grateful Dead** (then Warlocks) became house band for those multimedia events.
- Later West Coast rave/festival economies professionalized psychedelic party forms. Lineages into mega-EDM are mostly **commercialization**, not a single secret handoff from the Acid Tests to EDC.

{{illust:lab-beaker|A beaker that will not sit still — research upstream of the dance floor.}}

## Three acts

**Act I — Lab keys.** Cold War curiosity about mind-altering compounds put volunteers in rooms with clipboards. Kesey’s generation is the hinge: research access, then cultural explosion.

**Act II — The Tests.** Prankster theater, strobes, tape loops, Electric Kool-Aid, Dead as sonic substrate. Garcia would later talk about improvisation as simultaneous discovery—the band learning to be a single nervous system.

{{illust:prankster-bus|Furthur on a dithered highway — dashed lines racing under the wheels.}}

**Act III — Markets.** Warehouses become brands. Brands become festivals. Festivals buy insurance. Real harms (overdoses, predation) deserve case-based attention without requiring a cryonics subplot (see lesson 06).

{{illust:festival-scale|Crowd density under stage light — scale as spectacle and risk.}}

### On MI-6 and foreign services

UK and other services had Cold War interest in psychedelics. That is a separate research thread. For the **Dead / Kesey / Bay Area** story, the best-documented institutional link is **U.S. academic/hospital research access**, then culture. Do not substitute "MI-6" as a one-word explanation without specific primary evidence.
