## What this lesson is for

`VISION.md` notes that the boot sequence scrolls Rosary text and Psalms, that files carry ASCII art of Jesus and the Templar cross, and that the README closes with "Make a joyful noise" — while the CONTRIBUTING pitch stays religiously neutral so the project reads as *welcoming weird* rather than gated by belief. Two audiences, two doors, one house.

This tract is written for exactly that structure. This final lesson names the doors plainly:

- **The Christian door:** creation as participation in God's ongoing work. He constantly creates, building newer, bigger, better things at an exponential rate. What you build is participation in that.
- **The secular door:** compounding as the mathematics of anything that produces. Real growth is exponential. What you build is a system that produces more than it consumes.

Neither door is locked. Both open onto the same room: **what you build is the way out.**

---

## The transcript prompt

Open `tools/transcript-prompt.md`. It is a private reflection, not a share card:

1. Which door did I start from — the Christian or the secular?
2. What did I build?
3. Did the other door open on its own?

The prompt exists because the two-door structure is not a choice you make once. It is a structure you can move between. You may enter through one door on a Tuesday and find the other open on a Thursday. That is not confusion. That is the design.
