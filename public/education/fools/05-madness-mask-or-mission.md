## Content note and safety

This lesson discusses mental illness and punitive psychiatry. The track never diagnoses historical persons in its own voice. It never commends self‑harm or provoked death. If distress is acute, talk to someone you trust or a professional.

## Why five readings?

- Performance (Ivanov): the *yurodivy* is a role constructed with an audience.
- Illness: speculative if not tied to evidence; retrospective diagnosis is contested.
- Imputed madness: punitive psychiatry mirrors feigned folly — the state falsely ascribes madness to erase dissent.
- Chosen folly: the tradition insists the madness was assumed to hide sanctity and fight pride.
- Legend: late Lives and edifying edits are real; tag them.

Church suspicion inside the sources:

- Council in Trullo (692), canon 60 penalizes feigned possession.
- Moscow authorities grew hostile; recognition narrowed (Fedotov/Pravmir).
