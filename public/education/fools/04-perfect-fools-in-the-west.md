# 04 — Perfect fools in the West

> **Track:** Fools for Christ — Holy Foolishness, Endurance, and Love on Display · **Lesson 4 of 6**
> **Tools shipped with this lesson:** `tools/claim-sorter.md`

---

## What this lesson is for

Test whether the West had “holy fools” in the same sense, or only cousins. Avoid flattening East and West into one thing.

Named positions:

- P2 Western Catholic spirituality: folly as joyful humiliation (Franciscan and mystical strands).
- P3 Academic-historical: resist retrofitting Eastern categories onto Western texts; handle sources case‑by‑case.
- P1/P2 contrast: Eastern feigned madness vs. Western voluntary abjection without a formal fool rank.

---

## Francis of Assisi (primary texts VERIFIED; classification CONTESTED)

- Stripping before **Bishop Guido**: Thomas of Celano’s First Life (DOCUMENTED). The bishop covers Francis with his own mantle.
- “Perfect joy”: *Little Flowers* ch. 8 — patient endurance of insult and beating “for love of Christ” (DOCUMENTED).
- Reading used here: cousin tradition — joyful humiliation; not an Eastern‑style feigned madman (classification NOT ADJUDICATED; attribute when equating).

---

## Others frequently named

- Jacopone da Todi (c. 1236–1306): Franciscan poet; tomb said to call him *stultus propter Christum* (inscription itself UNVERIFIED). [Catholic Encyclopedia — SUPPORTED as report]
- Benedict Joseph Labre (1748–1783): canonized **8 Dec 1881**; lice‑ridden pilgrim beggar in Rome (DOCUMENTED status; Catholic Encyclopedia).

Saward’s thesis (SUPPORTED): the West has “perfect fools” as spirituality — not a formal rank paralleling Russian *yurodivye*. [John Saward, Perfect Fools, OUP 1980]
